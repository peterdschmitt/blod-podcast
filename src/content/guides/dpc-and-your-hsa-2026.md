## What changed

Before 2026, the IRS treated a DPC membership as a second health plan. If you had one, you generally couldn't put money into an HSA, even with a qualifying high-deductible plan.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup>

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act removed that conflict for a "direct primary care service arrangement" that meets a fee cap and covers primary care only. IRS Notice 2026-5 spells out the details.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup>

## Paying from your HSA

HSA money can now pay a qualifying DPC fee directly. With every individual-market bronze and catastrophic plan now HSA-compatible, this is what makes the "DPC plus a high-deductible plan" stack work.<sup><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup>

## If your employer pays

An employer-paid DPC fee is excluded from your taxable income, but you can't also reimburse that same fee from your HSA.
