# Case catalog (DPC)

A running list of every real, sourced case research turns up: studies, employer and public programs, state rules, published practice prices and patient stories. Every daily run adds what its research finds, and posts draw from here (the DPC copy of Decision Layer's `docs/use-case-catalog.md`).

**Labels:** **independent result** (third-party or peer-reviewed), **self-reported** (a program, employer or practice reporting its own numbers), **vendor-reported** (a DPC company or network's own claims), **survey** (a trade group or researcher's survey), **published price** (a practice's posted price list), **patient story** (a named person's account, in the press or online), **unverified** (seen only second-hand). Keep thin items in with their label; drop only what is fabricated or contradicted.
